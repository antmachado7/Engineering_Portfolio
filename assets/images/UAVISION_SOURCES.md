The ELANUS image was added on 21 October 2026. Programme recognition is described
as five finalists from more than 140 European entries, as published by UAVision.
The European Defence Agency confirms UAVision's participation in Sentinel Strike:
https://www.eda.europa.eu/what-we-do/research-technology/sentinel-strike-challenge

The images provide product context. They are not before-and-after documentation
of the portfolio author's mechanical redesign. The 23% total jammer assembly
weight reduction is the author's reported contribution, as recorded in the CV;
it is not a performance claim sourced from UAVision's public product pages.

The site loads resized WebP versions (960 pixels wide) of these images. The
Featured projects card uses a responsive four-image montage within the same
16:10 media area as the other cards. Original JPEGs are retained as sources.
