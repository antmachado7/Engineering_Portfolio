About

Engineering background.

Mechanical engineer specialised in structural analysis and composite materials, with experience in finite element modelling, bonded joints, and experimental validation. My work includes the numerical modelling of low-velocity impact damage in Glass/PPS composite laminates and the structural design of CFRP components for Formula Student electric race cars.

Background

Academic and project experience spanning aeronautical structures coursework at FEUP, a master's research thesis at Politecnico di Milano, and two seasons of Formula Student prototype vehicle development.

Strong working exposure to static, fatigue, impact, thermal, and non-linear simulation workflows, supported by testing, post-processing, and design iteration.

Master's thesis · Politecnico di Milano

Low-velocity impact modelling of Glass/PPS laminates

Abaqus/Explicit impact model with Hashin and cohesive-zone strategies, correlated against drop-weight impact response and ultrasonic C-scan measurements.

  • Delamination area predicted within about 15% of C-scan data
  • Bi-phasic shell-solid strategy used to study indentation behaviour
