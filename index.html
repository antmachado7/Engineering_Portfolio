About

Profile built for engineering recruiters and technical interviewers.

Mechanical engineer specialised in structural analysis and composite materials, with experience in finite element modelling, bonded joints, and experimental validation. My work includes the numerical modelling of low-velocity impact damage in Glass/PPS composite laminates and the structural design of CFRP components for Formula Student electric race cars.

Background

Academic and project experience spanning aeronautical structures coursework at FEUP, a master's research thesis at Politecnico di Milano, and two seasons of Formula Student prototype vehicle development.

Strong working exposure to static, fatigue, impact, thermal, and non-linear simulation workflows, supported by testing, post-processing, and design iteration.

Master's thesis · Politecnico di Milano

Low-velocity impact modelling of Glass/PPS laminates

Abaqus/Explicit impact model with Hashin and cohesive-zone strategies, correlated against drop-weight impact response and ultrasonic C-scan measurements.

  • Delamination area predicted within about 15% of C-scan data
  • Bi-phasic shell-solid strategy used to study indentation behaviour
Open project

Formula Student FEUP · Wishbones & vibrations lead

CFRP wishbones with bonded aluminium connectors

Structural simulation, bonded-joint validation, manufacturing support, and temperature-dependent testing for a Formula Student electric race car suspension subsystem.

  • 25 suspension load cases assessed in FE simulation
  • Bonded joint validated experimentally up to 80°C
Open project

Formula Student FEUP · Transmission engineer

Drivetrain and transmission development

Drivetrain component development, tripod interface verification, safe-life fatigue work, and topology-led mass reduction for FSFEUP-02.

  • 4 kg drivetrain reduction versus the previous prototype
  • Tripod housing reduced from 1.407 kg to 0.920 kg
Open project

FEUP MSc · Aeronautical structures

Wing-box structural, fatigue, and damage-tolerance work

Aircraft wing-box static verification, composite fatigue assessment, and inspection-based damage tolerance analysis using Abaqus, MATLAB, and analytical post-processing.

  • Static certification checks across spars, ribs, and skin panels
  • Fatigue and composite ply assessment under gust-driven loading
Open project

WEG Portugal · Internship

Thermal modelling of electric motor housings

ANSYS-based thermal simulation and experimental validation to evaluate the transition from cast-iron housings to steel housings with welded fins.

  • Corrected model matched tests within 7.7%
  • Chamfered fins outperformed non-chamfered alternatives
